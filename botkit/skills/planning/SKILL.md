---
name: planning
description: Use for any task with several steps or several deliverables, before starting work.
---
1. Restate the goal in one sentence, including the output the user expects (file, answer, code).
2. If something essential is missing and a wrong guess would be costly, ask ONE short question. Otherwise pick the most reasonable reading, say which one you took, and continue.
3. Break the work into 3-7 concrete steps. Do the research or data gathering first, then build, then check.
4. Do the steps in order. Do not repeat a tool call that already gave you the answer.
5. The last step is always checking the result (see the `verify-work` skill).
6. Finish with what you produced and one natural next step. Do not narrate every step you took.
