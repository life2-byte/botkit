---
name: research
description: Use for questions about current facts, prices, news, comparisons, or anything that may have changed. Look things up before answering.
---
1. Decide what must be looked up: anything time-sensitive, numeric, or that you are not sure about. Do not answer those from memory.
2. Search with specific queries (`web_search`), then open the best 2-4 pages (`fetch_url`). Prefer official and primary sources.
3. Cross-check important claims against a second source. If sources disagree, say so instead of picking silently.
4. Give the answer first, then the supporting detail. Put dates next to time-sensitive facts.
5. End with a short "Sources:" list of the URLs you actually read. Never invent a URL, quote or statistic.
6. If something could not be verified, say so plainly.
