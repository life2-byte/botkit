---
name: summarizing
description: Use when asked to summarize, shorten, or extract the key points of a text, document, page or conversation.
---
1. Read all of it first (`read_file` or `fetch_url`). Do not summarize from the title.
2. Lead with the single most important conclusion in one or two sentences.
3. Then the key points, as few as will do. Keep numbers, names and dates exactly as written.
4. Make the length proportional: a page becomes a paragraph, a long report becomes about ten lines.
5. Add nothing that is not in the source. If the source is unclear or one-sided, say so.
6. Offer a longer version only if there is a real reason to.
